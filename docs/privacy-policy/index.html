Reconnect Cable Guide

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Reconnect Cable Guide is designed to work offline.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share setup names, notes, serial numbers, photographs, purchase history, cable details or connection details from the app.

Local Storage

Setup data, device details, cable details, reconnect progress and photos are stored locally on the user's device. Photos selected or captured in the app are saved inside the app's local storage and are not uploaded by Reconnect.

Photos, Camera and Face ID

Reconnect uses the camera only when the user chooses to photograph devices, ports or cables for a setup guide. Reconnect uses the photo picker only when the user chooses specific images for a setup guide. Reconnect uses Face ID only when the user enables optional app lock.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Reconnect checks Apple-verified purchase entitlement status to unlock paid features.

Third Parties

Reconnect does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs or a remote backend.

Contact

For support, email admin@vensofts.com.
